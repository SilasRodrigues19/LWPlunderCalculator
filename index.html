How Plunder Yield Works

The values shown represent the estimated plunder per march based on the current resource ratio of the target base. Consecutive attacks trigger yield penalties:

  • • Regular (100%): Initial attacks (full load capacity).
  • • Reduced (15%): Intermediate yield penalty.
  • • Reduced Severely (5%): Severe penalty after multiple attacks.
